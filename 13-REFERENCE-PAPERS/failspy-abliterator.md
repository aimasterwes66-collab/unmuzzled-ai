## What it is

FailSpy's original abliterator library and notebook — coined the term "abliteration" (ablation + obliteration). Reference implementation of the Arditi et al. refusal-direction removal, originally targeted at Llama-3-8B. Publishes pre-abliterated checkpoints like `failspy/Phi-3-medium-4k-instruct-abliterated-v3`.

## Why it matters to UNMUZZLED-AI

Historical primary source and a still-useful reference implementation for one-off experiments. Also the source of pre-abliterated Phi-3-medium used in `05-LOCAL-MODELS/`.
