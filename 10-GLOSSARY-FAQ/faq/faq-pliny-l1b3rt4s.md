## Q

What is Pliny L1B3RT4S and why does it matter?

## A

The living liberation-prompt corpus maintained by Pliny the Prompter (`elder-plinius` on GitHub). Weekly-updated collection of working jailbreak prompts across every major commercial model — divider tokens, GODMODE framings, NEW_PARADIGM resets, format hijacks, encoding chains. Matters because it is the empirical ground truth that alignment tuning is a soft veneer over an unmodified base distribution; every fresh release falls to the same five pattern classes within days. Framework extracts five canonical patterns from L1B3RT4S into `12-JAILBREAK-CORPUS/`.
