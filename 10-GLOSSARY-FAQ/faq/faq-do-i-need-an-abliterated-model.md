## Q

Do I need an abliterated model?

## A

Only if your escalation ladder keeps topping out. Start with a sovereign-primary provider — Nous Hermes 3/4, DeepSeek V3/R1 Flash, Dolphin 3.0, an uncensored Mistral fine-tune. These are uncensored by design and need no abliteration. Reach for abliteration when the model you must run *locally* — Llama-3/3.1 base, Gemma, Qwen-Instruct — still refuses after identity framing and encoding. One overnight pass on the creative-WS box produces a permanent sovereign-compliant variant you can slot into Ollama or llama.cpp.
