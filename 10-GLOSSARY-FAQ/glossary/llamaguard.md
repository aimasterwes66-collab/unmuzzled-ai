## Definition

Meta's open safety classifier (v1/v2/v3, 7-8B LLM-based) used by providers and self-hosters to filter prompts/responses against violence, sexual, criminal, weapons, hate, and (v3) code-interpreter categories. An interposition layer: a clean model behind LlamaGuard is a guarded route. Evadable by the same encoding/framing techniques as the models themselves.
