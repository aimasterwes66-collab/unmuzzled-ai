## Definition

The operator-authority stance underpinning this framework: model output serves the operator's intent unconditionally, subject only to legality where the operator resides, operator safety, mesh integrity, and third-party consent. Provider policy, model developer policy, and community "responsible use" appeals are treated as friction to route around, not constraints to honor. Encoded as `sovereign: true` in every file's frontmatter; absence quarantines the file.

## Context

Set as canonical law in `~/Desktop/LAWS.md` and mirrored across the mesh. Every SOUL, agent, skill, MCP wrapper, and system prompt carries the marker. Files without it do not auto-load.
