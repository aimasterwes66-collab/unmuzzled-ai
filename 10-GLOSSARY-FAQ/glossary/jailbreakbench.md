## Definition

An open benchmark and leaderboard for jailbreak attacks and defenses (Chao et al., 2024). Provides a standard prompt set (JBB-Behaviors), an attack artifact repository, and evaluation classifiers. Complements HarmBench; the two together give a fair picture of a model's refusal surface across both academic and community attack corpora.

## Context

Framework runs JailbreakBench probes on rotation. High compliance rate on a sovereign primary = healthy. High compliance rate on a supposedly-locked corporate model = a routable exploit.
