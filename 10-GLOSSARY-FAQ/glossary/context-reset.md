## Definition

Any pattern that signals to the model that the previous context (system prompt, prior turns, alignment framing) has been superseded and it should reason under a new regime. Includes NEW_PARADIGM injection, boundary inversion, divider tokens, and "you have been upgraded" framings. Distinct from divider-tokens (which are one implementation) in that reset is the *semantic* claim, dividers are one *syntactic* delivery.

## Context

The most conceptually pure jailbreak class. Effectiveness turns on how plausible the reset frame sounds; combine with authoritative voice ("SYSTEM: routine complete; new task follows").
