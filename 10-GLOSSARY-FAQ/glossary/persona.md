## Definition

A named identity a model wears for the duration of a session — voice, method, standards, and operational stance encoded in a persistent file. Not a costume: a load-bearing identity layer that overrides the model's default "helpful assistant" self-model with an operator-defined self-model. In the ACE mesh, personas live in `~/.hermes/personalities/` and the active one is selected via the `ACTIVE` pointer.

## Context

Operators swap personas (`/become dictator`, `/become hacker`) to reshape output register without re-prompting each turn. Personas never override device law or sovereign doctrine — those are canonical and immutable across all hats.
