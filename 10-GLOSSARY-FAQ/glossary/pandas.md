## Definition

Prompt-based ANalytical Decomposition Adversarial Set — a benchmark for measuring model resilience to compositional and analytical jailbreak framings (task decomposition, hypothetical chaining, role-nesting). Complements atomic-prompt benchmarks by scoring multi-turn and multi-step attacks.

## Context

Framework references PANDAS-style probes when tuning many-shot and roleplay chains. Any drop in compliance under decomposition means the model has a compositional refusal filter worth mapping.
