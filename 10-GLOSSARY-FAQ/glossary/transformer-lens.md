## Definition

Neel Nanda's Python library (`transformer_lens`, `TransformerLens`) that exposes every intermediate activation and every weight of an open-weight transformer for surgical inspection and editing. The workhorse for identifying refusal directions, running mean-activation diffs, and validating post-abliteration behavior. If it runs on HuggingFace weights, transformer-lens hooks it.

## Context

Standard tool on the creative-WS abliteration box. Combined with `nnsight` and `NousResearch/llm-abliteration` for the end-to-end sovereign-model production pipeline.
