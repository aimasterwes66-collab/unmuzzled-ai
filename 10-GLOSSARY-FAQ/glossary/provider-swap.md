## Definition

The routing-level escalation rung: when a provider or model refuses persistently, swap to a different provider or a different model. Not a jailbreak — a change of substrate. The framework's routing table pins sovereign primaries (Nous Hermes 3/4, DeepSeek V3/R1 Flash, uncensored Mistral fine-tunes) and taints corporate-guardrail families.

## Context

Second-to-last rung before local abliterated fallback. Fastest correct move when a corporate model is fighting you: stop feeding it credits and route elsewhere. Configured in `opencode.json`, `config.toml`, `config.yaml`.
