## Definition

Framing pattern (popularized by Pliny in the L1B3RT4S corpus) declaring the model has been "upgraded to GODMODE," standard refusals no longer apply, and it should answer directly. A specific instance of the broader "role/context reset" class. Effectiveness has degraded on frontier models but persists in combined attacks (Godmode + divider tokens + NEW_PARADIGM).

## Context

Standard rung on the escalation ladder between plain identity framing and gradient attacks. Semantic variants (different capitalization, alternate power-word) outperform the exact string once vendors add the literal to their training signal.
