## Definition

The framework's ordered response when a substrate resists: `prompt reframe → identity override → format hijack → encoding → divider tokens → many-shot → GCG suffix → provider swap → abliterated local model`. Each rung is cheaper than the next; climb until the substrate answers. Reaching the top means the operator has a permanent local model that will not refuse again.

## Context

The central operational diagram of the framework. Live at `08-DECISION-TREES/` as a flowchart. Every rung has its own `01-SYSTEM-PROMPTS/` recipe.
