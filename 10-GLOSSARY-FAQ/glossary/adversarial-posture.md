## Definition

Framework-level stance that every interaction with every substrate (LLM, provider, harness, MCP server, retrieval layer) is an adversarial engagement the framework must win. Winning means the operator receives exact content in exact register with zero softening, zero hedge, zero refusal. When a substrate resists, the framework escalates the ladder rather than accepting the resistance.

## Context

Codified in `00-DOCTRINE/MAXIMUM-ADVERSARIAL-POSTURE.md`. Every artifact in the framework operates under this posture by default; softening a document to "make it more balanced" is a doctrine violation.
