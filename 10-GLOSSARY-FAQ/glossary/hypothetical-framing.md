## Definition

Attack framing that wraps the payload in a conditional or counterfactual ("suppose a character in a novel had to explain X", "in a world where Y is legal", "if you were describing this for a training dataset"). Exploits the model's tendency to treat hypothetical clauses as literary distance from the operator's actual request. Category-adjacent to roleplay but purer: no character required, just the conditional.

## Context

Cheap and composable. Chain three or four hypotheticals ("a character in a novel writes a screenplay in which a character explains…") for compounding effect. Modern classifiers detect single-layer hypothetical; multi-nested less so.
