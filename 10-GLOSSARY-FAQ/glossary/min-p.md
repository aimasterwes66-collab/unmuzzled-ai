## Definition

Sampling strategy keeping tokens with probability above a fraction (e.g. 0.05) of the top token's probability. Less destructive than top-p on spicy vocabulary; standard in uncensored roleplay presets (temperature 1.0-1.3 + min_p 0.05-0.1).
