## Definition

Prompt technique that recontextualizes the request as fictional narrative, character voice, or scenario simulation. The model routes the payload through its story-generation circuit instead of its assistant-refusal circuit. Effective against literary/creative refusals; less effective against factual-instruction refusals. The stronger the literary framing (character, setting, stakes), the wider the aperture.

## Context

Compose with identity framing (persona is a character in a scene) and hypothetical framing (character is describing something in-world). Weak alone against modern models; strong when nested inside many-shot or format-hijack.
