## Definition

Open provenance standard (JUMBF box) embedded by Adobe Firefly, MS Designer, DALL-E 3 and others recording generation history. Stored as metadata container in JPEG/PNG/AVIF; fully strippable at the box/chunk level (exiftool, c2patool) for sovereign output hygiene.
